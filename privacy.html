Beast Kitchen Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026. Uğur Kılıç publishes Beast Kitchen.

What stays on your iPhone

Beast Kitchen v2 works without a Beast Kitchen account. Your kitchen profile, including any body weight you enter, and your shopping lists, prep progress, timers, fridge and freezer records, meals eaten, and prep history are stored on your iPhone. The app uses weight and your goal on the phone to estimate a daily protein target. The app's own feature-use counters also stay on your phone. The optional online features share the specific information described below.

Optional online coach and Fridge Scan

When online features are available, the app asks permission separately for scanning photos and choosing meals online. You can type ingredients and build a Box on your phone. In You → Help & settings → Reset AI permissions, you can clear your choices so the app asks again before a future online request.

  • Fridge Scan. Take a photo or choose one from your library. After you allow sharing, the app sends a smaller JPEG copy to our Supabase function. The function sends the image to OpenAI or Anthropic, depending on which service is configured, to identify ingredients. The app shows a list you can check and edit. Use a photo of the food and keep people, addresses, and documents out of it.
  • Choose meals online. With your permission, the app sends your meal-planning goal, weekly budget if entered, number of people, available equipment, foods to avoid, any confirmed ingredients, and a shortlist of recipes to our Supabase function. The AI provider receives your goal and the shortlist to suggest three recipes. Your body weight, photo, and meal log are outside this request.
  • Request records. Supabase receives a randomly generated installation ID and request ID and, when available, your RevenueCat App User ID. We use these to check Premium and enforce usage limits. Our coach database stores request and installation IDs, the type and timing of requests, processing counts and estimated costs, and a coded version of the RevenueCat ID with subscription-access information where applicable. Our coach function does not save photos, request text, or AI replies in the coach database.

The AI provider processes the image or meal details to answer the request. Its handling and retention can depend on the service and its settings. Supabase hosts the function and database. See the Supabase Privacy Policy, OpenAI Privacy Policy, and Anthropic Privacy Policy.

Subscriptions and purchases

Apple handles checkout, payment, and subscription management. We do not receive your card details. RevenueCat checks access to Premium and restores purchases. It receives purchase and subscription information, a randomly assigned app user ID, and technical details such as an Apple vendor device identifier when available and the app and iOS versions. Our coach may ask RevenueCat to verify Premium using that app user ID. See the Apple Privacy Policy and RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Notifications, sharing, and ads

If you allow notifications, your iPhone schedules prep timers, prep-day reminders, and fridge reminders on the device. You can turn them off under Settings → Notifications → Beast Kitchen.

When you open a Walmart product search, your browser sends the item name to Walmart. If you share a Box, you choose where to send it using the iPhone share sheet. Those services apply their own privacy policies. Beast Kitchen does not sell personal information, display ads, or use your information to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Deleting data and contacting us

You → Help & settings → Start over clears your kitchen profile and records in the app. Deleting the app removes its local kitchen data from that iPhone. A local reset or uninstall does not cancel an Apple subscription or remove records already held by our service providers.

To ask about, correct, or request deletion of online records, including coach usage records or RevenueCat subscription records, email truespanmedia@gmail.com. Tell us which feature you used so we can help identify the relevant records. We use your email and the details you provide to answer and handle your request. Apple handles its own payment records. Please leave out passwords, payment details, and sensitive photos.

Beast Kitchen is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13.
