What comes with Premium?

Premium opens the paid Boxes and lets you build a Box from the full recipe library. The Starter Box is free. Open Premium in the app to see the current yearly and weekly prices. Apple shows your exact price, renewal terms, and any trial you qualify for before you confirm.

How do I restore my subscription?

Use the Apple Account that bought the subscription. In Beast Kitchen, go to You → Help & settings → Restore purchases. You can also tap Restore purchases on the Premium screen.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Beast Kitchen → Cancel Subscription. You can also open Apple's subscription settings through You → Help & settings → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or subscription renews to avoid another charge. Deleting Beast Kitchen does not cancel your subscription.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles App Store billing and refund decisions. Sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase at Apple's Report a Problem page to request a refund.

How do I delete my data?

There is no Beast Kitchen v2 account to delete. Go to You → Help & settings → Start over to clear your kitchen profile, prep progress, and fridge records. You can also delete the app to remove its local kitchen data. The separate Reset AI permissions option clears your choices about future photo and meal-planning requests when online tools are available.

For help requesting deletion of online coach usage or RevenueCat records, email truespanmedia@gmail.com and tell us which feature you used. Apple manages its own payment records.
